We are pleased to submit our manuscript, "Structure-Based Virtual Screening of FDA-Approved Drugs and Molecular Dynamics Simulation Identifies Predicted Ligands of the Pan-Cancer Multivitamin Transporter SLC5A6 (SMVT)," for consideration as a research article in Cell Reports.

## Summary

The sodium-dependent multivitamin transporter (SMVT/SLC5A6) is a cell-surface metabolite transporter positioned at the intersection of vitamin uptake and lipogenic metabolism. We provide the first comprehensive, multi-dimensional pan-cancer analysis of SLC5A6—integrating transcriptomic profiling of 10,967 TCGA samples, survival analysis, mutation constraint scoring, protein–protein interaction networks, pathway enrichment, and structure-based virtual screening of 3,311 FDA-approved drugs followed by 100-ns molecular dynamics simulations.

## Key Findings

1. **Expression-driven cancer target**: SLC5A6 is significantly upregulated in 14 of 21 cancer types with matched normal tissue, yet harbors only passenger-level somatic mutations—confirmed by ESM-2 pathogenicity analysis (two-model cross-validation, Spearman ρ = 0.81) and structural mapping demonstrating that even strongly destabilizing variants avoid the biotin substrate site.

2. **Unexpected pharmacophore**: Structure-based screening identifies barbiturates as high-affinity SMVT ligands (phenobarbital −8.30 kcal/mol, exceeding biotin −6.76), with the barbituric acid core mimicking biotin's ureido ring. Expanded screening reaches a glutarimide record of −9.36 kcal/mol, with CF₃-substituted glutarimides providing a clear structure–activity relationship for lead optimization.

3. **Drug-repurposing roadmap**: MD simulations (100 ns, 8 compounds) confirm stable binding of all virtual-screening hits; cross-method validation (Vina ↔ MD ↔ MM-GBSA) is concordant for most compounds, flagging furosemide (Vina-over-optimistic) and the negative control riboflavin as outliers requiring experimental confirmation.

4. **Provocative metabolic axis**: 5-hydroxytryptophan (5-HTP), the serotonin precursor, is a top-ranking aromatic-amino-acid candidate and engages the SMVT biotin substrate site (pose overlap 0.37 Å; 11.2-ns MD confirms tight binding with the lowest backbone RMSD—0.49 Å—of all nine simulated complexes)—suggesting SMVT may function as a blood–brain barrier 5-HTP transporter, with NSAID-mediated SMVT inhibition providing a potential mechanistic link between chronic NSAID use and depression risk.

## Significance

SMVT is druggable (gabapentin enacarbil is an FDA-approved SMVT-targeted prodrug), and its substrate-recognition cavity is pre-evolved for small-molecule binding. Our work establishes SLC5A6 as a pan-cancer metabolic vulnerability and provides both a computational framework and a validated hit series for developing barbiturate- and glutarimide-scaffold SMVT-targeted anticancer agents.

## Fit for Cell Reports

This study provides a systems-level, data-rich analysis of a previously understudied cancer target—combining pan-cancer genomics with structure-based drug discovery in a single framework. The transporter-focused repurposing strategy (barbiturates, glutarimides, 5-HTP) and the experimentally testable SMVT–serotonin axis hypothesis offer immediate translational follow-up opportunities, consistent with Cell Reports' emphasis on accessible, reproducible findings with broad biological and therapeutic relevance.
