## Result 1 — SLC5A6 is not a cell-autonomous essential gene anywhere (usable, and it constrains our wording)

DepMap `CRISPRGeneEffect`, n = 1,150 models with a score.

- mean gene effect **+0.015**, median **+0.023** (≈ neutral; convention ≤ −0.5 = common essential)
- **1 of 1,150 lines (0.1%)** at or below −0.5
- no lineage reaches the threshold: most-negative means are Testis −0.134 (n=4),
  Vulva/Vagina −0.114 (n=2), Ovary −0.018 (n=60), **Bowel −0.017 (n=61, 1.6% of lines)**,
  Breast −0.015 (n=51), Lung −0.006 (n=125) — full table in
  `slc5a6_crispr_by_lineage.csv`

Reading: knockout of SLC5A6 does not kill or arrest cancer cells in suspension
culture. Whatever makes SLC5A6-high tumours vulnerable is **conditional** — most
plausibly biotin/pantothenate availability in vivo, which a plastic flask does not
model. This is a genuine limitation of the "metabolic vulnerability" framing, and
reviewers with DepMap access will find it. Say it before they do.

## Result 2 — unbiased expression–response screen is non-specific (report as exploratory, do not feature)

286 drugs tested (≥30 lines each), Spearman of SLC5A6 log2(TPM+1) vs AUC
(higher AUC = less sensitive, so ρ<0 means SLC5A6-high → more sensitive),
Benjamini-Hochberg across all 286. 179,104 drug×line rows usable; 458 rows lost to
unmapped Sanger IDs.

- **27 of 286 drugs at FDR<0.05** — 17 on the sensitive end, 10 on the resistant end
- effect sizes are small throughout: |ρ| between 0.11 and 0.32
- sensitive end: AZD5991 −0.180 (apoptosis/BH3 mimetic), Sorafenib −0.175, BI-2536
  −0.170 (cell cycle), Eg5_9814 −0.167, POMHEX −0.149 (PLK1), Vorinostat −0.137
  (HDAC), LGK974 −0.135 (WNT/palmitoleoylation)
- resistant end is **dominated by EGFR-axis inhibitors**: Dasatinib +0.315 (strongest
  single association), AZD3759 +0.164, JNK Inhibitor VIII +0.139, Gefitinib +0.137,
  Sapitinib +0.125, Erlotinib +0.112

Reading: the signal is lineage/proliferation confounding (EGFR-driven lines, cell
cycle/apoptosis agents), not multivitamin transport. Nothing here implicates the
biotin–ACC–FASN axis. Honest label: **negative/exploratory**.

## Result 3 — the one pre-registered mechanistic candidate is null

CPI-637 (devimistat) is the only screened compound with a documented link to an
SMVT substrate axis (lipoamide/lipoate). Registered before looking, because the
resistant-end hit list is otherwise a fishing trip.

- n = 571, **ρ = +0.018, p = 0.663, FDR = 0.80** → no association.
